Withdrawals — who may take seed out, how much, and what happens to anything that comes back.

**Business meaning**
A withdrawal spends the collection. Every rule here exists to make the spending
visible and bounded, because a vault that cannot say what it gave away is a
warehouse.

**Who may authorise**

| Role | May authorise | Ceiling per request |
|------|---------------|---------------------|
| Registrar | research distribution | 50 seeds |
| Curator | research, breeding, repatriation | 250 seeds |
| Director | anything, including base collection | no ceiling |

**Rules**

- No single lot may give up more than 10 % of its remaining mass in a rolling
  twelve months, whoever authorises it.
- Requests are served from the working sub-sample first. Reaching the base
  collection needs the Director and is recorded as a *depletion event*.
- A lot on a `watch` verdict (`viability-testing`) may be withdrawn only by a
  Curator, and never for distribution.
- **Returns are never re-shelved.** Seed that leaves the vault and comes back is
  quarantined for 45 days and then accessioned as new material
  (`accessions`) or destroyed. It does not rejoin its parent lot.
- Repatriation requests — a community asking for its own material back — skip
  the ceiling and the rolling limit, and are the only category that may be
  served from a Tier 3 lot without a Director's signature.
