# Analytics vocabulary

- "Falloff" means funnel exit rate: for each pages.funnel_step (1 landing, 2 product, 3 pricing), the share of sessions whose last pageview (exited_here=1) was on that step.
- "Views" are pageviews rows; "sessions" are visits; bounce means a one-page session.
- Revenue excludes refunded rows unless refunds are the question.
- Week-over-week comparisons use viewed_at/occurred_at dates.
