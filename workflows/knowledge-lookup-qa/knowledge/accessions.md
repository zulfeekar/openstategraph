Accessions — how a seed lot enters the vault, and what it must carry before it is given a lot id.

**Business meaning**
An accession is the moment a bag of seed becomes a *record*. Before it, the
material is a donation; after it, the vault has promised to keep it alive and
to be able to say where it came from.

**The five things an accession must carry**

| Field | Meaning | Refused without it |
|-------|---------|--------------------|
| Collector record | who gathered it, when, and at what coordinates | yes |
| Taxon | genus and species as accepted by the reference list | yes |
| Provenance class | wild, landrace, cultivar, or breeding line | yes |
| Mass | grams of clean seed after threshing | yes |
| Permit | the collecting or transfer permit reference | wild and landrace only |

**Rules**

- A lot id is `MSV-<year>-<five digits>` and is issued once. A re-accessioned
  lot after regeneration (`viability-testing`) gets a **new** id and a pointer
  to its parent; ids are never reused and never edited.
- Minimum viable accession is 500 clean seeds for a wild provenance class and
  1,500 for a cultivar. Below the minimum the material is held as a *donation*
  and is not a lot.
- Quarantine on arrival is 21 days at Tier 1 conditions for every accession,
  and 45 days for anything with a permit reference from outside the region.
- Every accession enters at Tier 1 (`storage-tiers`) and is first tested at
  6 months.
